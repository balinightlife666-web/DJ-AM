About Anya Kaizy

Female DJ from Bali with a versatile, energetic all-genre approach designed to adapt to different audiences, moods and event concepts.

Anya Kaizy is a female DJ who began her musical journey in 2023, bringing a versatile and energetic presence to every performance. With the ability to explore and blend various genres, Anya Kaizy creates dynamic sets that can adapt to different audiences, moods, and event concepts.

Throughout her journey, Anya Kaizy has performed at various events including bazaars, weddings, gatherings, and graduation celebrations. Her versatility and ability to connect with the crowd allow her to deliver an engaging musical experience for every occasion.

With a passion for music and continuous growth behind the decks, Anya Kaizy continues to develop her identity as a DJ and bring fresh energy to every stage she steps onto.
